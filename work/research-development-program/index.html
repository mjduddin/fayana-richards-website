Research and Professional Development Program for Underrepresented Students

University of Massachusetts Amherst Post-Doctoral Research Associate January 2018 – January 2019

Project Context

During her postdoctoral appointment at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, affiliated with the Center for Community Health Research, Fayana contributed to community-engaged research on chronic disease prevention and transnational health.

Need

The center sought to expand research opportunities and professional development for students from underrepresented backgrounds, and to prepare a competitive National Institutes of Health (NIH) R01 grant application with sound staffing, budget, and compliance plans.

Goals

  • Build a structured research and professional development program for underrepresented students.
  • Support the preparation of an NIH R01 grant application.
  • Coordinate research and professional development activities across faculty, staff, students, and community partners.

Stakeholders

Faculty, staff, students, community partners, and the Center for Community Health Research.

Scope

Program design and coordination, grant application support (staffing, budget, and compliance planning), and community-engaged research support.

Responsibilities

  • Designed and implemented a professional development curriculum for students from underrepresented backgrounds, expanding access to research training and institutional support.
  • Supported NIH R01 proposal development by drafting, editing, and conducting compliance reviews of grant requirements and budget preparation.
  • Coordinated input across faculty and administrative stakeholders to ensure the timely, accurate, and compliant submission of a competitive funding proposal.
  • Collaborated with a Community Research Liaison and Project Manager to strengthen the Center for Community Health Research's Community Steering Board.
  • Supported community-engaged research on chronic disease prevention and transnational health, including targeted literature reviews.

Planning

Program development involved defining activities, participants, and coordination needs; grant support involved translating research aims into staffing plans, budgets, and compliance considerations.

Implementation

Fayana worked across faculty, staff, students, and community partners to coordinate activities and keep program development and grant preparation moving together.

Research and Data Methods

Community-engaged research practices, research administration, and program coordination.

Deliverables

  • A professional development curriculum for underrepresented students, designed and implemented
  • Drafting, editing, compliance-review, and budget contributions to an NIH R01 grant application

Risks and Constraints

Grant preparation involves firm deadlines and compliance requirements; the program needed to serve students well while remaining feasible for the center.

Results

  • Contributed to the timely, accurate, and compliant submission of a competitive NIH R01 funding proposal.
  • Designed and implemented a professional development curriculum that expanded access to research training and institutional support for underrepresented students.

The grant application's funding outcome is not published here pending verification.

Lessons

This role connected research administration with program design, sharpening skills in staffing plans, budgets, and compliance that carry directly into research program management.
