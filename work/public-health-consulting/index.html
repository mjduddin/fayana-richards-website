Case Study

Public Health Consulting and Program Coordination

Michigan Public Health Institute Senior Public Health Consultant June 2021 – 2025

Project Context

The Michigan Public Health Institute supports public health organizations, agencies, and communities through consulting, evaluation, and program services. Fayana served as a Senior Public Health Consultant from June 2021 to 2025, working across multiple client engagements at the same time.

Need

Client organizations needed help translating public health and social justice priorities into organized, well-managed programs — from initial intake and scoping through implementation, facilitation, and partnership management.

Goals

  • Define each client's needs clearly and convert them into actionable scopes of work.
  • Keep concurrent projects organized, on schedule, and visible to stakeholders.
  • Support clients in addressing racism, oppression, and health disparities through credible, well-facilitated programming.
  • Maintain strong, accountable partnerships across organizations.

Stakeholders

Client organizations, organizational leaders, internal consulting teams, supervised staff, and community and organizational partners.

Scope

Multiple concurrent public health consulting projects, including social justice programming, workshop design and facilitation, partnership support, and project planning and monitoring.

Responsibilities

  • Led or coordinated multiple concurrent public health projects and led a team of public health consultants.
  • Conducted client intake meetings and developed scopes of work, project plans, and work plans.
  • Established and monitored timelines and milestones, and tracked deliverables.
  • Coordinated internal and external stakeholders and supported community and organizational partnerships.
  • Designed and facilitated workshops, including virtual sessions addressing racism, oppression, health disparities, and related public health issues.
  • Supported contract negotiations, partnership agreements, and joint-venture agreement work.
  • Managed sensitive issues and escalations and advised organizational leaders.
  • Supervised staff and developed or maintained dashboards and project-tracking tools.

Planning

Engagements began with client intake meetings to clarify needs, goals, and constraints. Fayana translated those discussions into scopes of work and project plans with defined timelines, milestones, responsibilities, and deliverables, and she maintained tracking tools to keep progress visible.

Implementation

She coordinated internal and external stakeholders across concurrent workstreams, facilitated meetings and workshops, monitored deliverables against plans, managed sensitive issues and escalations as they arose, and adjusted plans based on new information.

Research and Data Methods

Used qualitative and quantitative information to support program decisions, including stakeholder feedback and project-tracking data.

Deliverables

  • Scopes of work and project plans
  • Workshops and facilitated virtual sessions
  • Dashboards and project-tracking tools
  • Advisory support to clients and organizational leaders

Risks and Constraints

Work involved sensitive public health topics, multiple concurrent commitments, and confidential client relationships, requiring careful issue management, escalation support, and disciplined documentation.

Results

Public outcome details are limited because the work involved confidential organizational information.

Lessons

Concurrent consulting work reinforced the value of early scoping, visible tracking, and direct, timely communication when sensitive issues arise.

Confidentiality

Client names, project names, contract information, and partner names are not published. Selected project details have been summarized to protect organizational confidentiality.
