Science Journalism and Research Translation

Science Magazine Editorial Intern Dates in review

Project Context

Fayana completed an editorial internship with Science Magazine, working across written and audio formats to translate technical research for broader audiences. The dates of this role are being confirmed against approved records.

Need

Science journalism requires accuracy, clarity, and speed: technical research must be verified, distilled, and communicated in ways general audiences can trust and understand.

Goals

  • Translate technical research accurately for broad audiences.
  • Maintain rigorous fact-checking standards.
  • Produce engaging digital and audio science content.

Stakeholders

Editors, researchers, and the magazine's readership.

Scope

Editorial research, interviewing, fact-checking, digital writing, and podcast production.

Responsibilities

  • Interviewed researchers and conducted background research.
  • Fact-checked scientific information.
  • Wrote articles for the Science website.
  • Produced a podcast episode.
  • Supported digital science communication.

Planning

Each piece began with background research and interviews to establish an accurate foundation before writing.

Implementation

Fayana developed stories through interviews and verification, then produced articles and audio content for digital publication.

Research and Data Methods

Interviewing, background research, fact-checking, science writing, and podcast production.

Deliverables

  • Articles for the Science website
  • A produced podcast episode

Risks and Constraints

Science communication carries a duty of accuracy; errors damage both the publication's and the researcher's credibility.

Results

Specific article and podcast details are not published here pending verification. Verified outcome information will be added after review of approved project records.

Lessons

Fact-checking and research translation remain central to how Fayana communicates evidence to executives, communities, and general audiences today.
