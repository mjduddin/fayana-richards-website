Federally Funded Research Project Coordination

University of Arizona Project Coordinator 2009 – 2010

Project Context

Early in her career, Fayana served as Project Coordinator for a federally funded research project at the University of Arizona from 2009 to 2010.

Need

Federally funded research requires disciplined coordination: schedules, documentation, financial monitoring, data management, and supervision must all run reliably for the project to meet its obligations.

Goals

  • Keep project activities coordinated and documented.
  • Monitor expenditures and provide accurate monthly expense reporting.
  • Support sound data management and undergraduate research supervision.

Stakeholders

The project's research team, undergraduate researchers, and university administration.

Scope

Day-to-day project coordination, financial monitoring and reporting, data management support, and supervision of undergraduate research activities.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinated activities for a federally funded research project.
  • Supported biweekly budget reviews with the Principal Investigator and coordinated with the Business Office Manager.
  • Prepared monthly expense reports and monitored project expenditures.
  • Conducted qualitative and quantitative data transcription and analysis.
  • Supervised and collaborated with undergraduate research assistants on research design, analysis, and presentation development.
  • Presented research findings at national conferences.

Planning

Coordination work centered on keeping schedules, documentation, and reporting cycles organized and predictable.

Implementation

Fayana managed monthly reporting rhythms, tracked expenditures, supported data management, and supervised undergraduate researchers' day-to-day activities.

Research and Data Methods

Project documentation and reporting, expenditure monitoring, and qualitative and quantitative transcription and analysis using MAXQDA and SAS, with reference management in EndNote.

Deliverables

  • Monthly expense reports
  • Project documentation and reporting
  • Supervised undergraduate research activities

Risks and Constraints

Federal funding carries reporting and stewardship obligations that demand accuracy and consistency.

Results

The project's total funding amount is not published here pending verification. Verified outcome information will be added after review of approved project records.

Lessons

This early role built the financial monitoring, documentation, and coordination habits that underpin Fayana's later program management work.
