Employment June 2021 – 2025

Senior Public Health Consultant

Michigan Public Health Institute

Senior consulting role spanning multiple concurrent public health projects. Combined project leadership, client advising, facilitation, partnership support, and evidence-informed decision-making, with a focus on social justice programming and health equity topics.

  • Coordinated six concurrent projects and workstreams, aligning timelines, deliverables, and stakeholder communication with organizational and client priorities.
  • Led weekly team meetings and individual check-ins for project teams of four to nine members, preparing agendas, directing action-item follow-up, reviewing milestones, and assigning responsibilities.
  • Conducted client intake meetings, developed and monitored scopes of work, and supported contracts, partnership agreements, and joint-venture agreements through issue identification and follow-up.
  • Managed production of deliverables including toolkits, concept papers, a statewide strategic plan, workshop series, and facilitation guides for grant agencies, public health professionals, older adults living with disabilities or dementia and their caregivers, and government officials.
  • Designed and facilitated more than 50 workshops, including virtual sessions addressing racism, oppression, health disparities, and related public health issues.
  • Submitted quarterly progress summaries and final reports to funding agencies, collaborative partners, and clients, and co-monitored budgets with the Senior Financial Analyst.
  • Supervised and mentored two staff members and reviewed work produced by research scientists and research and evaluation associates.
  • Co-wrote five concept papers with collaborative partners for grant opportunities.
